The report in 30 minutes

Go through the steps in order (the minutes are in the bar at the top). Learn the yellow phrases. Then press Hide phrases, say each blank out loud and tap it to check.

1 · 3 min

Structure: 250 words (225–275)

HeadingWhat goes in itwords
HeaderTo: · From: · Date: · Subject: (not counted, no “Dear”)–
IntroductionAim = present + analyse + recommend, then the source40
Results4–5 figures, biggest first, each with a phrase and a linking word75
Analysis2–3 possible reasons (may, likely, suggests)50
Recommendations3 measures, impersonal, each with a reason55
ConclusionMain finding + main recommendation. Nothing new.30

Always use exactly these five headings. Then you can’t be accused of prompt lifting in a heading.

2 · 10 min

The template: learn the yellow, fill in the blue

To: person from the task
From: Leon
Date: 13 November 2026
Subject: Report on … (own words)

Introduction

The aim of this report is to present the results of a survey on …, to analyse them and to make recommendations on how to …. The report is based on a survey conducted among 1,100 people aged 14–21.

Results

According to the survey, X is by far the most popular option, chosen by almost all respondents (96%). This is closely followed by Y (85%). A large proportion of respondents … (70%), while around two thirds …. In contrast, only a small minority … (5%).

Analysis

Several factors may explain these results. One possible reason for this is that …. Another important factor is …. This suggests that ….

Recommendations

In order to attract more … / improve …, it would be advisable to …, as reason. Furthermore, the school / company should consider …-ing. Another possible measure would be to ….

Conclusion

In conclusion, main finding. However, with main recommendation, the company could attract a significant number of new customers.

Spare phrases, so you don’t repeat yourself

  • Data: The results show that … · The data indicates that … · The chart illustrates that …
  • Linking: Moreover, · In addition, · However, · Nevertheless, · whereas · By comparison, · As a result,
  • Analysis: This may be due to … · It is likely that …
  • Recommend: It would be beneficial to … · One possible solution would be to …
  • Trend (only if the chart shows years): rose sharply / fell slightly / remained stable / reached a peak of · a sharp rise · a slight decline

5 · 5 min

Model: about 260 words (shopping centre task)

Read it once, slowly. It is the template filled in.

To: The Centre Manager
From: Leon
Date: 13 November 2026
Subject: Making the shopping centre more appealing to young people

Introduction

The aim of this report is to present the results of a survey on why young people visit the shopping centre, to analyse these findings and to make recommendations on how to increase the number of young visitors. The report is based on a survey conducted among 1,100 people aged 14 to 21.

Results

According to the survey, meeting friends is by far the most common reason, mentioned by almost all respondents (96%). This is closely followed by shopping for brands (85%). A large proportion of young people also come to eat out (70%), while around two thirds use the free internet (66%). Free entertainment is the least popular reason, although it was still chosen by 60%.

Analysis

These figures suggest that the centre is mainly a social meeting point rather than just a place to shop. One possible reason for this is that there are few other places where teenagers can spend time together at little cost. Another important factor is the large number of affordable fast-food restaurants, which are popular with young visitors.

Recommendations

In order to appeal to a younger audience, it would be advisable to create comfortable seating areas, as friends could meet there without having to buy anything. Furthermore, the centre should consider adding healthier food options and trendy cafés. Another possible measure would be to organise regular events such as concerts or gaming tournaments.

Conclusion

In conclusion, young people visit the centre mainly to socialise. However, with more meeting spaces and a wider range of food and entertainment, the centre could attract a significant number of new visitors.
